Disclaimer: This calculator is a clinical planning aid only and does not replace clinical judgement or local SOPs. All outputs are estimates based on nominal cylinder capacities (±5%) and flow rate settings (±20%). Changes in patient condition, FiO₂, minute volume, circuit leaks, inline nebulisers, or journey delays (traffic, weather, vehicle breakdown) will alter actual oxygen consumption. Always verify cylinder pressure gauges and valve operation physically prior to departure and carry an adequate emergency reserve.
